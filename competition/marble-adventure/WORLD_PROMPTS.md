# Marble Adventure — World Prompts (hero regeneration pack)

**Purpose**: Regenerate the 8 portal worlds behind the gates so each is a
showpiece. Each prompt is Marble-optimized (spatial layout, scale, lighting,
texture density, atmosphere) and written in the style proven by the community
gallery's top entries.

**Usage**: `competition/regenerate_worlds.ps1` reads these prompts. Each
generation costs **1500 credits** (marble-1.1). After regeneration, update the
`marble_id` in `data/portals.json` and re-run `download_cdn_thumbs.ps1`.

**Model**: marble-1.1 (fixed 1500 credits) for all 8. Seeds suggested per
world for deterministic regeneration.

---

## 1. gothic_cathedral — "Vaulted stone and rose light"

> A vast gothic cathedral interior at dusk. Towering rib-vaulted stone ceilings
> receding into darkness 60 metres above the nave floor. Three massive
> stained-glass rose windows on the east wall casting deep ruby, sapphire and
> amber light shafts through slowly drifting dust motes. Rows of slender
> clustered columns with carved foliate capitals flanking the main aisle,
> receding in strict perspective toward the altar. A carved stone altar at the
> eastern terminus with a single flickering votive candle. Worn flagstone
> floor with irregular slabs and shallow depressions worn by centuries of
> footsteps. Deep side chapels with pointed arched openings revealing shadowy
> alcoves. Flying buttresses visible through tall lancet windows on the south
> wall. Atmospheric haze in the upper vaults. Cool stone greys and warm amber
> light. Profound sense of scale and silence.

**Seed suggestion**: 45219

## 2. cyberpunk_alley — "Rain-slick neon backstreet"

> A claustrophobic urban alleyway at midnight during heavy rain. Walls on both
> sides rising 8-10 storeys, lined with rusted fire escapes, exposed ductwork,
> and mismatched window units — some lit with warm tungsten, others dark.
> Saturated cyan and magenta neon signs casting sharp coloured light onto wet
> pavement. Deep puddles with oil-slick rainbow sheens reflecting the neon
> glow. A single flickering red tube sign at the far end. Steam rising from a
> grate near the centre. Discarded newspapers matted against a drain grate.
> Narrow enough to touch both walls with outstretched arms. Dense volumetric
> fog illuminated by the neon. High contrast — near-black shadows in corners,
> saturated colour where light hits. Cinematic anamorphic lens feel, shallow
> depth of field towards the distant end.

**Seed suggestion**: 88731

## 3. sea_of_fog — "Mist over silent valleys"

> An ocean of low fog filling a vast valley at dawn, viewed from a high rocky
> ridge. The fog forms a perfectly flat, glowing white sea that stretches to
> the horizon, pierced by dark mountain islands and isolated forested peaks.
> The nearest rocky outcrop is a weathered granite crag 40 metres below the
> viewpoint, its base swallowed by the fog. A few gnarled wind-bent pines grow
> from cracks in the rock. The rising sun is low and golden, casting long
> diagonal light shafts across the fog sea and painting the mist in warm
> apricot and silver tones. The upper slopes of the mountain islands catch
> direct golden light. The fog surface has subtle texture — slow rolling
> waves, wisps rising like steam, occasional holes revealing a dark forested
> valley floor far below. The air is cold, still, and silent. Near-pastel
> palette: cream fog, slate blue mountains, amber sunlight.

**Seed suggestion**: 11308

## 4. wonderland — "Floating bioluminescent islands"

> A chain of seven floating islands suspended in a twilight sky at varying
> altitudes, connected by slender stone arch bridges. The largest central
> island is 80 metres across with a grassy plateau, a single enormous
> bioluminescent tree whose branches drip with glowing teal and violet
> lantern-fruits, and a ring of standing stones at its centre. Waterfalls
> cascade from two islands, falling 100 metres into a luminous mist. The
> undersides of every island glow with dense fungal bioluminescence — teal,
> magenta and soft blue — reflecting onto the clouds below. Satellites and a
> crescent moon in the deep indigo sky. Small luminous spores drift lazily
> through the air. The bridges are ancient weathered stone with moss-filled
> joints, glowing lichen tracing their arches. Warm violet and teal palette
> against deep blue night.

**Seed suggestion**: 60427

## 5. japanese_temple — "Autumn maples and koi pond"

> An ancient Japanese temple courtyard in full autumn colour. A two-tiered
> wooden pagoda with dark lacquered timber and curved eaves rises behind a
> perfectly still koi pond. The pond reflects the blazing crimson and orange
> maples that surround it, their branches arching low over the water. Carp
> drift beneath the surface, flashes of gold and white. A weathered stone
> lantern sits at the pond's edge, moss growing up its base. Fallen maple
> leaves float on the water's surface and carpet the stone path. The temple's
> sliding shoji screens are open, revealing a dim interior lit by warm lantern
> light. A single stone bridge crosses the narrowest part of the pond. Light
> is soft and diffuse — late afternoon sun filtered through the maple canopy,
> casting dappled shadows on the moss. Deep reds, oranges, dark wood, and
> quiet water. Profound stillness and harmony.

**Seed suggestion**: 23950

## 6. deep_forest — "Redwood light and moss ruins"

> A cathedral-like redwood forest where colossal trees rise 80 metres,
> their trunks 4 metres thick with deeply furrowed rust-red bark. Between the
> trees stand the moss-covered ruins of a stone structure — collapsed
> columns, half-buried arches, and a broken statue swallowed by ferns. The
> forest floor is a dense carpet of sword ferns, sorrel, and decaying logs
> covered in bright green moss and shelf fungi. Long golden shafts of light
> pierce the canopy at sharp angles, illuminating dust and pollen motes and
> pooling on the forest floor in scattered patches. A thin mist hangs at knee
> height, catching the light. The ruins' stones are covered in a deep layer of
> moss, their carved details softened by centuries. The scale is disorienting
> — the trees dwarf the human-scale ruin elements. Deep green, rust brown,
> and amber light.

**Seed suggestion**: 77124

## 7. midcentury_villa — "Golden-hour modernist pool"

> A mid-century modernist villa at golden hour, seen from the pool deck. The
> single-storey house is a low horizontal composition of warm cedar cladding,
> floor-to-ceiling glass walls, and a flat roof with deep overhangs. The
> kidney-shaped pool in the foreground mirrors the amber sky, its surface
> glassy and warm. An Eames-style lounge chair and a low teak sunbed sit on
> the pool deck beside a sculptural floor lamp that hasn't been lit yet. A
> mature palm tree casts long shadows across the deck. Through the glass
> walls, the interior glows with warm indirect light — a sunken living room,
> a stone fireplace, built-in shelving. The sky is a gradient from deep gold
> at the horizon to pale amber and soft blue above. Late afternoon sun rakes
> across every surface, making the wood grain glow. Warm, calm, aspirational.
> Strong horizontal lines and generous negative space.

**Seed suggestion**: 33506

## 8. underwater_ruins — "Ruins under glowing sea"

> Ancient stone ruins on the ocean floor, 30 metres below the surface. A
> collapsed temple complex of weathered sandstone blocks covered in
> encrusting corals, sea fans, and waving kelp. A broken stone archway stands
> over a sandy path, its columns draped in glowing tube anemones. Sunlight
> filters down from the surface in long god-rays, illuminating suspended
> particles and casting drifting light patterns on the sand. Schools of
> silver fish sweep through the ruins. A towering statue of a forgotten deity
> lies toppled on its side, half-buried, coral growing from its outstretched
> hand. The water has a deep teal-blue colour with strong depth falloff into
> darkness beyond the ruins. Bubbles rise from a rocky vent near the base of
> the statue. Rays of light, swaying kelp, drifting sand — the scene feels
> alive and eternal.

**Seed suggestion**: 99218

## Regeneration checklist

1. `competition/regenerate_worlds.ps1` — generate all 8 (12,000 credits)
2. Copy each world's `marble_id` into `data/portals.json`
3. `competition/download_cdn_thumbs.ps1` — refresh portal thumbs
4. Open each world in the Spark viewer and spot-check quality
5. Verify in-game: `just marble-adventure-play`
