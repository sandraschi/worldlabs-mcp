## Marble Adventure

A small 3D hub with glowing portal rings. Walk through a ring — your **browser** opens a real **Marble** Gaussian splat world. Close the tab and return to the hub.

**Prototype** · World Labs Marble 1.1 worlds · Godot 4.4 hub

### No account needed

Worlds open at `marble.worldlabs.ai`. You do not need a World Labs account to play.

### Controls

- **WASD** — move
- **Mouse** — look
- **Scroll** — field of view
- **Space** — jump
- **H** — hub center
- **R** — reset tour progress
- **Esc** — release mouse

Tour **5 featured worlds**; **3 bonus** rings on the outer orbit.

### AI / tooling disclosure

World environments were generated with **Marble 1.1** (World Labs). The hub was built with **Godot 4.4**. This is a competition / portfolio prototype, not a commercial product.

### Requirements

- Windows 10/11
- Modern browser (Chrome / Edge recommended) for portal worlds
