## Findings (with evidence)

### CRITICAL (-15 each)

- C1 MCPB manifest uses ${PWD} — `mcpb/manifest.json:12,14-15`
  (`"args": ["run","--directory","${PWD}","run_server.py"]`, `"PYTHONPATH": "${PWD}/src"`).
  Claude Desktop does not expand ${PWD}; bundle will not start on a real install.
  Audit: `audit-mcpb-bundles.ps1 -Repo worldlabs-mcp` reports NONSPEC. Fix: ${__dirname}.
- C2 MCPB stdio entry is the HTTP sidecar — `mcpb/manifest.json:9`
  (`"entry_point": "run_server.py"`; run_server.py runs uvicorn when a port env is set).
  Desktop entry must be stdio (`python -m worldlabs_mcp`; `src/worldlabs_mcp/__main__.py`
  exists and delegates to `server:main`). Audit reports SIDECAR.

### HIGH (-8 each)

  exists (`web_sota/vite.config.ts:18-23`) but the app never uses it. Same failure
